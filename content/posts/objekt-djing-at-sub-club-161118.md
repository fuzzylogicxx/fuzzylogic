Really looking forward to checking Objekt at the Sub Club on Friday night. I’ve enjoyed his productions since the first and although I don’t like everything, when he’s good he’s _really_ good. This one from 4 years ago on a split 12″ with Dopplereffekt blew my tiny mind when it came out and remains one of my favourite techno records this decade. DJing-wise he seems to be pretty unpredictable, which is fine by me!
