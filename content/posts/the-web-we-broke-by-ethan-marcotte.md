[@beep](https://twitter.com/beep) writes: “WebAIM has published an accessibility analysis of the top one million home pages and the results are abysmal”.

To address the issue he recommends we start small (picking up new accessibility tips regularly) and work together.

I agree. The web is supposed to be universal. We developers – or more accurately developers, designers, managers i.e. all of us – need to up our collective game to get things back on track.

I’ve always been interested in web accessibility and tried to do my best… but I can do better. I’ve just subscribed to [David Kennedy’s A11y Weekly newsletter](https://a11yweekly.com/) and plan to work through some sections of [https://a11yproject.com/](https://a11yproject.com/) as soon as possible.
