Lyla, Noto, Tipo, Aizle. Stuart Ralston.

Drive a bit stressful – Edinburgh traffic a bit stressful. A lesson learned!

They knew it was our wedding anniversary and mentioned this at the top of the menu, gave us a warm welcome and a free glass of fizz – nice touches.

Decor and vibe lovely.

Food and drink:

Clair was driving but was happy for me to go for the wine pairing.

* Alp blossom cheese, onion, quince
* Lobster, kohlrabe, sake
* Laminated brioche, koji & wild garlic butter from Ampersand dairy
* Cured halibut, candied kelp, umai. Paired with [Dewazakura jumai ginjo sake](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/59963/dewazakura-dewasansan-junmai-ginjo).
* Scottish langoustine, burnt apple, sorrel. Paired with [Willi Schaefer feinherb reisling](https://www.tanners-wines.co.uk/products/graacher-riesling-feinherb-willi-schaefer-mosel-2021) 
* Sea trout, courgette, smoked pike roe. Paired with [Rafael Palacios louro do bolo](https://www.thegoodwineshop.co.uk/products/rafael-palacios-louro-godello)
* Partridge, radicchio, green peppercorn. Paired with [Arlaud, bourgogne rouge *oka* burgundy](https://www.vinvm.co.uk/domaine-arlaud-bourgogne-oka-2022)[](https://www.thegoodwineshop.co.uk/products/rafael-palacios-louro-godello)
* Laganory, ibiscus and flowered lavosh. Paired with [Domaine Fontanel, Maury Rouge](https://www.de-burgh.com/shop/Maury-Rouge-Grenat-VDN-Domaine-Fontanel-2021-p132330869)
* Amalfi lemon, yoghurt bronze fennel
* Plum, creme crue, sake. Paired with [Chateau tirecul la graviere, monbazillac sgn](https://ndjohn.co.uk/products/chateau-tirecul-la-graviere-monbazillac)
* Malt dulcey bon bon, and kelp & white chocolate financier

nice service including the somellier who hit the right balance of interesting without going too far, and Jade who had recently moved to Edinburgh from Glasgow southside. [](https://www.thewhiskyexchange.com/p/59963/dewazakura-dewasansan-junmai-ginjo)

Amazing sake the most memorable.
