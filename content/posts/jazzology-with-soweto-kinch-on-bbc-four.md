Loved this BBC documentary which traces jazz’s roots back to Congo Square in New Orleans, explores the origins of the word jazz, and explains the music’s cultural importance as a _language_ – spoken first by slaves – for self-expression and representing community.

My favourite section was the interview with pianist [Marcus Roberts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcus_Roberts) in which he explained how jazz came from the blues and described blues music in terms of “dissonance that resolves” and “tension and release… just like life”.
