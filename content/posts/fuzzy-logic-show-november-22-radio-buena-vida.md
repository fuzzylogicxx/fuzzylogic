Had a lot fun playing records live from the window of my local radio station.

This selection kicked off with some deep jazz and boogie, worked through the gears with brazilian sounds and electro, then finished with some heads-down house and techno.

[Listen to the show on Soundcloud.](https://soundcloud.com/radiobuenavida/fuzzy-logic-w-laurence-hughes-radio-buena-vida-191122)

Here’s the tracklist:

1. Lonnie Liston Smith - Saiz
2. Charles Stepney - On your face
3. Metropolis – Every time I see him
4. Kaidi Tatham – Don't Rush The Process
5. Admin – Step into light
6. Charles Stepney – Denim Groove
7. Thundercat – Thousand Knives (tribute to Ryuichi Sakamoto)
8. Eraan – Enough
9. Fudge Fingas vs. Vin Landers – Sum Background
10. Marcos Valle – Estrelar
11. Nahash – Terry
12. Gary Gritness – Lithium Polymer
13. Pépé Bradock – El Pescador
14. Mafikizolo – Loot

Thanks to Speedy, Susan and the RBV team for having me.
