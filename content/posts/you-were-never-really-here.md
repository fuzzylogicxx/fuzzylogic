Just watched _You Were Never Really Here_, directed by Lynne Ramsay and starring Joaquin Phoenix, with [Clair](https://clairirwinphotography.com). It’s hard-hitting and intense but really good. My favourite part was the opening 15 minutes which introduces Phoenix’s character _Joe_ and uses _Dark Streets_ from Johnny Greenwood’s excellent soundtrack to set the mood and tension perfectly.
