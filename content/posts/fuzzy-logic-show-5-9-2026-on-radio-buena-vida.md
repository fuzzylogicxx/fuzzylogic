In the run-up to this radio show I had recently moved house, so hadn’t had much time for new record shopping. Simultaneously I’m close to hitting the big half-century. So it felt timely to go for a theme rather than a selection of new records. 

Since I was feeling nostalgic I decided to anchor the mix around some early inspirations – two killer instrumentals from New Order and 808 State – and let it flow from there.

[Listen to the show on Soundcloud.](https://soundcloud.com/radiobuenavida/fuzzy-logic-w-laurence-2)

<!-- excerpt -->

Here’s what I played.

1. New Order – Confusion (instrumental) – Factory
2. Karen Young – Hot for you (dub two) – Sunshine

Fun fact: the last show before me ([Somewhere in between](https://soundcloud.com/radiobuenavida/somewhere-in-between-683652758)) finished with a foreign language version of David Bowie’s *Modern Love*… and the show on right after me *started* with *Modern Love* (original)! A wee bit of spooky, unplanned serendipity. 

Cheers to Speedy, Charlotte and the rest of the RBV crew for having me.
