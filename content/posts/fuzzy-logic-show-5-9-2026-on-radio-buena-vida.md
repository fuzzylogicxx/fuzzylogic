In the run-up to this radio show I had recently moved house, so hadn’t had much time for new record shopping. I’m also approaching the big half-century. So it felt timely to go for a theme rather than a selection of new records. Since I was feeling nostalgic I decided to anchor the mix around some early inspirations (New Order and 808 State) and let it flow from there.

Listen to the show on Soundcloud.

<!-- excerpt -->

2. Karen Young – Hot for you (dub two) – Sunshine
3. Benedek – Doodat - L.I.E.S.
4. Newcleus – Cyborg Dance – Sunnyview
5. Neil Landstrumm – Sahara – CPU
6. Candy Flip – Evolution – Debut
7. EVM128 – Fling Foot – Self-released
8. Plaid – Slice of cheese – Black Dog Production

10. The Speaking Clock – Kim was off sick – Self-released

12. Thelma Houston – You used to hold me (Dub) – MCA

14. Dee-lite – What is love? – Elektra
15. Telex – Raised by snakes – Razormaid

Fun fact: the last show in the station before me ([Somewhere in between](https://soundcloud.com/radiobuenavida/somewhere-in-between-683652758)) finished with a foreign language version of David Bowie’s *Modern Love*… and then the next show right after me *started* with *Modern Love* (original)! Spooky. 

Cheers to Speedy, Charlotte and the rest of the RBV crew for having me.
