Jeremy Keith suggests that it might be better if our websites ask people to grant permission for new content (e.g. blog posts or podcasts) to be _silently cached_, rather than asking permission to send intrusive notifications.

I’ve just implemented web push notifications on [the Greenhill Journal](https://www.greenhilldigital.com/journal/) so this is definitely food for thought.
