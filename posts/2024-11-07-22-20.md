For James’s 40th, Clair bought him the gift of some vouchers for a wild cooking and dining experience of [Wild Kabn Kitchen](https://www.wildkabnkitchen.com/) at Ardkinglas Estate, Cairndow near Loch Fyne. The idea was that James, Grant and us would go but with Grant looking after a recovering Toby, Toad stepped in.

I didn’t know much about Wild Kabn in advance and wasn’t prepared for how off the beaten track it was! Toward the end of the journey you’re driving along the world’s narrowest wee track and beating your way through tree branches, which all kind of adds to the excitement.

We were having the *Sunday lunch by fire* and, since it was a beautiful afternoon, we sat outside the greenhouse (right in the middle of the woodlands with mountains around us) beside two other couples. All three courses were fantastic but t. Lovely walk around afterwards, beach etc.

Bob

It was magic, thanks! We went to a wee foodie place Clair had earmarked. Drove out past the Rest and be Thankful and  Arrochar to Loch Fyne then into a wee area called Ardkinglas and into the woods to eat outside in the sun where a dude cooked local grub over fire 🔥. It was Clair’s pal James’s 40th and Toad came with us too.
