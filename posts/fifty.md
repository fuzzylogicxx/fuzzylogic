In the words of Ferris Bueller, ”life moves pretty fast“ and true enough, I’m now 50 years old. How did that happen?

I may no longer a spring chicken, but I’m healthy, happy and lucky, and I just had a lovely week of celebrating the milestone with family and friends.

<!-- excerpt -->

On Thursday night, we had a family dinner at Cibo on Victoria Road. Clair and I felt it was the perfect neighbourhood venue, and owner Michael is a brilliant host.

There were quite a few generations there – my mum and dad; me, Clair, Martin and Tricia; all ten of my wonderful nieces and nephews; and lastly their lovely kids. My father-in-law Gary came too, along with a handful of other relatives and close family friends. This made it feel really special.

It went great! Michael and team put on an excellent menu (complete with Italia 90 world cup theme – one of my strong memories as a kid – and everyone loved the food. I had cornetto di salmone, followed by Talgiatellle Rome Mia, then Tiramisu – delicious. My mum and dad have been really busy and must have been tired but they were in great form – as was everyone.

I got some generous presents – records and vouchers, whisky, and a newspaper from my date of birth among them. And as the lights were dimmed my mum delivered a Spider-man cake (harking back to my comic collector days) while everyone sang, which gave me a big smile and a great laugh.
