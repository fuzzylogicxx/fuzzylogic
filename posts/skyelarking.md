## I’ve just had the most life-affirming short break with wonderful friends, a stunning location, food, music and sunshine. I’m a lucky boy!

I’m just back from a trip to Skye with Clair, Gillian, Aarti and Ruth for the tenth anniversary of the [Skye Live festival](https://www.skyelive.co.uk/). 

After settling into our apartment at picturesque Portree Harbour on Wednesday afternoon we headed out for dinner at [Scorrybreac](https://scorrybreac.com/) and it was a proper treat. At an table we shared with some of the lovely festival team, we enjoyed the May tasting menu featuring delights like Mangalitza pork taco, Lochbay scallop (with fennel, bisque and Laphroig whisky) and the oh so tasty treacle soda bread with smoked date butter. I also met chef Calum, who is not only a talented, lovely and generous fella but a great laugh. We all ended up having a bit of a party, with Calum rolling out the big speaker.
